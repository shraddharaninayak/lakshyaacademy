OUR LEGACY

Shaping Maharashtra's Next Generation of Civil Servants

Established in 2010 in Nashik, Lakshya Career Academy is dedicated to guiding aspirants towards their goals in competitive examinations. Founded under the academic leadership of Atul Nikumbh, Nishant Pawar, and Pranjal Nikumbh, the academy focuses on quality education, structured preparation, and personalised academic guidance.

With experienced faculty, comprehensive study materials, regular practice, and a supportive learning environment, Lakshya helps students strengthen their knowledge, develop confidence, and prepare for the challenges of competitive examinations.

Our vision goes beyond examination preparation. We aim to nurture capable, responsible, and knowledgeable individuals who aspire to contribute to society through public service.

Systematic Methodology

The Lakshya 3 Stage Academic Blueprint

From preliminary MCQ precision to Mains analytical writing and Board interview readiness, our pedagogical system leaves nothing to chance.

Stage 01 • Foundation & Speed

Prelims Exam Mastery (पूर्व परीक्षा)

Comprehensive conceptual clarity across GS 1 and CSAT aptitude with 12-year PYQ weightage mapping and weekly 100-mark OMR speed drills.

  • 12-Year MPSC Topicwise Question Mapping
  • CSAT Logical Reasoning & Data Interpretation
  • Weekly Negative Marking Elimination Drills
  • State Board 5th to 12th Foundation Mastery
Stage 02 • Analytical Depth

Mains Descriptive Excellence (मुख्य परीक्षा)

Daily answer-writing practice corrected line-by-line by directors. Focus on high-scoring structure, administrative keywords, and case studies.

  • Daily Descriptive Answer Writing & Feedback
  • GS 1 to 4 Complete Syllabus Decomposition
  • Marathi & English Grammar & Essay Writing
  • Maharashtra Economic Survey & Budgets
Stage 03 • Leadership Presence

Interview & Board Guidance (मुलाखत)

Panel mock interviews conducted by retired and serving Class-1 officers, accompanied by video analysis, body language coaching, and DAF review.

  • Mock Panels with Serving & Retired Officers
  • Video Performance Review & Tone Correction
  • Detailed Application Form (DAF) Analysis
  • Psychological & Confidence Counseling
