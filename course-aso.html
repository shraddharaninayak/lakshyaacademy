Syllabus Matrix

All Core Subjects & Statutes Covered

• Current Affairs: National and international events with high relevance to state governance and cabinet resolutions.
• Intelligence & Reasoning: Mental ability, logical deduction, situational analysis, and numerical problem solving.
• Right to Information Act - 2005 (RTI): Statutory provisions, appellate authority roles, public information officer responsibilities, and cases.
• Indian Constitution & Constitutional Amendments: Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles, Parliamentary procedures, and judiciary.
• State Government & Secretariat Administration: Mantralaya rules of business, inter-departmental file routing, and executive governance in Maharashtra.
• District Administration & Revenue Hierarchy: Collectorate authority, division commissionerates, and field implementation mechanisms.
• Local Self-Governance & Panchayati Raj: 73rd & 74th Constitutional amendments, Zilla Parishad, Panchayat Samiti, and Municipal Corporations.
