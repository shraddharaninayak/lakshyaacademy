About Lakshya Career Academy

Welcome to Lakshya Career Academy, Nashik's premier coaching institute for recruitment exams conducted by MPSC. We are committed to helping aspirants achieve their career goals by providing them with the best guidance and resources.

Our coaching institute specializes in preparing students for various MPSC exams, including the Civil Services Exam (ACP/DYSP/Assistant Commissioner Sales Tax/Tahasildar/Deputy Collector/PSI/STI/ASO, etc.) Examinations. We have a team of experienced and knowledgeable faculty members who use innovative teaching methods to make learning an enjoyable and empowering experience for our students.

At Lakshya Career Academy, we believe in providing a supportive and inspiring learning environment to help students achieve success in their careers. Our comprehensive study material, regular mock tests, and individual attention to each student ensure that they are thoroughly prepared for their exams.

We take pride in our students' success and have helped numerous aspirants realize their dreams of joining the civil services. Our commitment to excellence and dedication to our students have made us one of the most trusted coaching institutes in Nashik.

Whether you are a first-time aspirant or looking to enhance your skills, Lakshya Career Academy is the right place for you. Join us and let us help you achieve your career aspirations.

Strategic Directive

Our Mission

Bridging the Representation Gap & Empowering Maharashtra's Youth

01

The Reasons: Maharashtra Lags Behind in Competitive Tests Because…

Students from Maharashtra have made tremendous progress in the corporate field but their presence in Government Administration, especially at the helm, is negligible. Maharashtra contributes to 10% national population but less than 7% to its administration.

To add to this, young boys and girls from the state are lured by medical, engineering, and other lucrative fields. The luster of the media also attracts them. It is imperative that Maharashtra should have a definite say in the national administration and in national affairs. The UPSC and MPSC provide a vast scope and candidates should take constructive advantage of this opportunity.

02

Administrative Power of Maharashtra Vis-à-Vis Other States

Delhi, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Bihar, Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh from North, and Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Kerala from South send more than 70% of their youth for the Competitive tests to serve either state or central cadre.

Maharashtra should, in fact, send many more candidates and strengthen its cadre. These candidates are well versed with the local problems and hence may be the right people to govern their own state and champion development from within.

03

Preparing the Policy Makers & Able Administrators

It is an Administration that draws out developmental plans. The actual implementation of these plans spells the development and prosperity of the State or Nation. Though Maharashtra is a progressive state and a lot has been done in that direction, still it is not sufficient.

In that context, the people mostly conversant with the State’s problems, alone, can tackle them. The aim of Lakshya IAS Academy is to provide the best UPSC & MPSC classes and take concentrated efforts to prepare these students who are conversant and able administrators by giving them appropriate guidance.
